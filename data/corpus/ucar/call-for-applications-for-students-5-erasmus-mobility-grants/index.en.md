## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS FOR STUDENTS | 5 ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANTS

The University of Carthage is launching a call for applications for its students for five (5) Erasmus+ mobility grants at the Università degli Studi Internazionali di Roma (UNINT), Italy, for the Spring Term of the 2026/2027 academic year.

📅 **Application deadline: 15 October 2026**

👉 Please refer to the document below for detailed information on the application requirements, selection criteria, and required documents:
