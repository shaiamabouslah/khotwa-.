## CALL FOR APPLICATIONSAN ERASMUS+ MOBILITY SCHOLARSHIP AT ALANYA ALAADDIN KEYKUBAT UNIVERSITY (ALKU)(Turkey)

Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program allows students to participate in mobility at a foreign institution to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

Target Audience:
– Students of the University of Carthage enrolled in the following fields: Economics, Business Administration, Tourism Management, Food Engineering, English Language and Literature

Mobility Duration:
Five months during the 2025/2026 academic year.

Number of Scholarships:
One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is offered to students of the University of Carthage.

Host University:
Alanya Alaaddin Keykubat University: https://alanya.edu.tr/

Eligibility Requirements:
– Be enrolled in one of the University of Carthage institutions
– Have a B2 level in English or Turkish
– Obtain approval for the mobility project from the academic supervisors
– Submit the application within the specified deadline

Application Procedure:
Interested candidates must submit their application files to the University of Carthage’s Registry Office by 02/01/2026.

Selection Criteria:
– Motivation
– Academic merit
– Language skills

Application File:
– Proof of enrollment
– Motivation letter
– Updated CV
– Language certificate at B2 level
– One letter of recommendation
– Academic transcripts (from high school onwards)
– Copies of diplomas
– Valid passport copy
– Mobility project (Learning Agreement) validated and signed by the Director and Head of Studies or Department Head
