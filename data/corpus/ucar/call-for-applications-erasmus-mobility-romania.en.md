## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS – ERASMUS+ MOBILITY (Romania)

The University of Carthage is launching a call for applications for two (2) Erasmus+ mobility grants at Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași (Romania), intended for the academic staff of the University of Carthage.

📅 **Application deadline: 16 October 2026**

📎 Applicants are invited to carefully review the Call for Applications attached below for detailed information on the eligible fields, participation requirements, selection criteria, and required documents.
