## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS TWO ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANTS AT THE UNIVERSITY OF SEVILLE (SPAIN)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program enables participants to undertake a mobility period at a foreign institution in order to acquire new skills and competencies. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

### Target Audience

- - Administrative and technical staff of the University of Carthage.

### Duration of the Mobility

Five days between **May 11 and May 23, 2026**.

### Number of Grants

Two (02) **Erasmus+ mobility grants** are offered for administrative staff.

### Conditions

Applicants must:

- - Be an administrative staff member at one of the institutions of the **University of Carthage**.

- - Have a good level of **English**.

- - Have their **mobility plan approved by the administrative supervisors**.

- - Submit the application file **within the specified deadline**.

### Application Procedure

Interested candidates must submit their **application files to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage before March 19, 2026**.

### Selection Criteria

- - Motivation

- - Academic merit

- - Language skills

### Application File

The application must include:

- - Certificate of employment.

- - Motivation letter.

- - Updated CV.

- - Copy of a valid passport.

- - **Mobility project (Training Agreement)** approved and signed.

**Note:** All required documents must be **written in English**.
