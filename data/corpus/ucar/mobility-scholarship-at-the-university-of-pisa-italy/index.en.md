## MOBILITY SCHOLARSHIP AT THE UNIVERSITY OF PISA (ITALY)

**Program Overview**
This mobility opportunity is part of the MARE Project (Mobility and Research in the Mediterranean), a Transnational Education (TNE) initiative funded under the Italian National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Code TNE23-00049.

The project aims to strengthen university cooperation, promote student and staff exchanges, and foster joint research activities among Mediterranean universities. Through this mobility program, participants will have the opportunity to experience high-quality academic environments, strengthen international partnerships, and contribute to the development of key thematic areas such as Agriculture, Veterinary Sciences, and Engineering. This initiative offers students and academics a unique opportunity for professional and academic development within the Mediterranean region.

Within this framework, the University of Carthage is launching this call for applications for mobility opportunities with the University of Pisa for the academic year 2025/2026.

**Target Audience:**

- Students enrolled in Master’s or PhD programs at the University of Carthage in the following fields: Agriculture, Veterinary Sciences, and Engineering.
- Academic and administrative staff of the University of Carthage.

**Duration of Mobility:**

- For students: 6 months during the 2025/2026 academic year.
- For staff: 14 days during the 2025/2026 academic year.
  All mobility periods must be completed by **March 31, 2026**.

**Number of Scholarships:**

- Two mobility scholarships are available for students from the University of Carthage.
- Two mobility scholarships are available for staff from the University of Carthage.

**Application Procedure:**
Interested candidates must submit their application files to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage **no later than May 9, 2025**.

**Selection Criteria for Students:**

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language proficiency
- CV and extracurricular activities

**Selection Criteria for Staff:**

- Motivation
- Purpose of the mobility
- Language proficiency
- Teaching program content

**Student Application File:**

- Application form
- Transcripts of previous university studies
- English language certificate (B2 level)
- CV in English
- Mobility project (Learning Agreement) validated and signed by the Director and Head of Studies (or Department Head)
- Copy of passport
- Motivation letter
- Enrollment certificate for the academic year 2024/2025

**Staff Application File:**

- Application form
- CV in English
- Mobility project
- Copy of passport
