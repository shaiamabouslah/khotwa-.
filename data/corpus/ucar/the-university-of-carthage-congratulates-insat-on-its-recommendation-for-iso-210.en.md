## The University of Carthage congratulates INSAT on its recommendation for ISO 21001 certification

The University of Carthage is pleased to announce the recommendation of the Institut National des Sciences Appliquées et de Technologie (INSAT) for **ISO 21001 certification**, a recognition that highlights the institution’s ongoing efforts to strengthen the quality of its educational management system.

This achievement reflects INSAT’s strong commitment to excellence, institutional performance, and continuous improvement in the service of its students and stakeholders.

The University of Carthage extends its congratulations to the entire INSAT community and wishes them continued success in pursuing their development and excellence objectives.
