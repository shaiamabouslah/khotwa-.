## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS – ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANT AT CUKUROVA UNIVERSITY (TÜRKİYE)

The University of Carthage is pleased to announce **one Erasmus+ mobility grant** at **Cukurova University (Türkiye)**.

🎓 **Field of study:**
Agriculture, Biological and Related Sciences (Biotechnology) – Fisheries

📅 **Mobility period:**
Fall Term 2026/2027

📍 **Host university:**
Cukurova University – <https://www.cu.edu.tr/>

🎯 **Eligibility:**
Students enrolled at the University of Carthage (Master or PhD level) with a minimum B2 level in English and a validated mobility project.

📅 **Application deadline:** 22 June 2026

📄 **Application documents and details:**
• Call for Applications: <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tTuJOhqA4veHwtGU9QZbqEPFJODMcuOw/edit>
• Learning Agreement for Studies: <https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-CkXJvR3gr4RR3WN2UmJuZmV6tJeqBD6/edit>

📌 Applications must be submitted to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage.

📧 For further information, please contact the Erasmus+ Program Office at the University of Carthage.
