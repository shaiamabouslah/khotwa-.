## CALL FOR APPLICATIONSONE ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANT AT THE UNIVERSITY OF TRENTO (ITALY)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program offers the opportunity to undertake mobility at a foreign institution in order to acquire new skills. This mobility period is validated and recognized by the home institution.

### Target Audience

Administrative staff of the University of Carthage. The training week will focus on international mobility and scientific research.

### Duration of the Mobility

Five days.

### Number of Grants

One Erasmus+ mobility grant is offered for staff of the University of Carthage.

### Host Institution

University of Trento: <https://event.unitn.it/isw2026/>

### Eligibility Conditions

- - Be an administrative staff member in one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.

- - Have the mobility plan approved by the administrative authorities.

- - Submit the application file within the specified deadline.

- Have a B2 level in English or Italian.

### Application Procedure

Interested administrative staff must submit their application file to the registry office of the University of Carthage before **20/02/2026**.

### Selection Criteria

- - Motivation

- - Academic merit

- Language skills

### Application File

- - Employment certificate

- - Motivation letter

- - Updated CV

- - Copy of a valid passport

- - Mobility project (Training Agreement)

All required documents must be written in English.
