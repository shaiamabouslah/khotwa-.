## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS — ERASMUS+ MOBILITY SCHOLARSHIP AT THE UNIVERSITY OF TRENTO (ITALY)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program offers the opportunity to undertake a mobility stay at a foreign institution to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

### Target Audience

Faculty members from the University of Carthage teaching in the field of **Engineering and Computer Science**.

### Duration of Mobility

- **Five (5) days**

### Number of Scholarships

- **One** Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is available for faculty members of the University of Carthage.

### University of Trento Website

<https://international.unitn.it/incoming/university-of-carthage>

### Eligibility Requirements

- Must be a **faculty member** at one of the institutions affiliated with the University of Carthage.
- Must have at least **B2 level proficiency in English or Italian**.

### Application Procedure

Interested faculty members must submit their application documents to the **University of Carthage’s central registry office** no later than **May 23, 2025**.

### Selection Criteria

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language proficiency

### Application File

- Employment certificate
- Motivation letter
- Updated CV
- Copy of valid passport
- Mobility project (**Teaching Agreement**)
