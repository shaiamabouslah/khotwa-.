## Call for applications two erasmus+ mobility scholarships at the universitat autonoma de barcelona (spain)

**Program Overview**
The Erasmus+ program enables mobility to another institution abroad to acquire new skills. This mobility is recognized and validated by the home institution.

**Target Audience:**
Administrative staff of the University of Carthage.

**Mobility Duration:**
Five days during the 2025/2026 academic year.

**Number of Scholarships:**
Two Erasmus+ mobility scholarships are offered for the target audience of the University of Carthage.

**Host University:**
Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona: [www.uab.cat](http://www.uab.cat)

**Conditions:**

- Be administrative staff at one of the University of Carthage institutions.
- Have a good level of English.
- Validate the mobility plan with the Administrative supervisors.
- Submit the application file within the given deadline.

**Application Procedure:**
Interested administrative staff must submit their application file to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage before **October 24, 2025**.

**Selection Criteria:**

- Motivation.
- Academic merit.
- Language skills.

**Application File:**

- Online application form: <https://eformularis.uab.cat/web/ari/application-form_staff-in-mobility_project-2024>
- Certificate of employment.
- Motivation letter.
- Updated CV.
- Copy of a valid passport.
- Mobility project (Training Agreement) validated and signed.

*All requested documents should preferably be written in English.*
