## Call for applications Erasmus+ mobility grant at Alanya Alaaddin Keykubat University (ALKU) – Turkey

**Program Overview:**
The Erasmus+ program allows students to undertake mobility at a foreign institution to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

**Target Audience:**

Doctoral students enrolled at the University of Carthage in the following fields: Economics, Business Administration, Tourism Management, Food Engineering, English Language and Literature.

**Mobility Duration:**
Five months during the 2025/2026 academic year.

**Number of Scholarships**:
One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is offered to students of the University of Carthage.

**Host University:**
Alanya Alaaddin Keykubat University: <https://alanya.edu.tr/>
