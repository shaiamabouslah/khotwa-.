## AN ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANT AT THE UNIVERSITY OF GRANADA (SPAIN)

## Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program allows students to carry out a mobility period at a partner institution abroad to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

## Target Group

Students from the University of Carthage enrolled in Bachelor’s or Master’s programs in the following disciplines:

<https://www.ugr.es/universidad/campus>

## Mobility Duration

Autumn Term of the academic year 2025/2026.

## Number of Grants

- One Erasmus+ mobility grant is offered for Tunisian students.

## Host University

University of Granada

## Mobility Offer for Administrative Staff

<https://internacional.ugr.es/estudiantes/movilidad-entrante>

## Eligibility Requirements

- Be enrolled at one of the institutions of the University of Carthage
- Have at least a B2 level in English or Spanish
- Have the mobility project approved by academic supervisors
- Submit the application file within the deadline
- Master’s students must obtain a pre-admission (see annex)

## Application Procedure

Interested candidates must submit their application files to the General Registry Office of the University of Carthage no later than **May 2, 2025**.

## Selection Criteria

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language proficiency

## Application File

- Certificate of enrollment
- Motivation letter
- Updated CV
- Language certificate (B2 level)
- One letter of recommendation
- Academic transcripts (starting from the Baccalaureate)
- Copies of diplomas
- Copy of valid passport
- Mobility project (Learning Agreement) approved and signed by the Director and Head of Studies or Department Head

*All documents must be written in English or Spanish.*
