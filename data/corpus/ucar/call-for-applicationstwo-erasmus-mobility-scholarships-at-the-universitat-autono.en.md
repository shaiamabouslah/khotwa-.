## Call for applications two erasmus+ mobility scholarships at the universitat autonoma de barcelona (spain)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program allows students to undertake a mobility period at a partner institution abroad to acquire new skills. This mobility is recognized and validated by the home institution.

### Target Audience

Students of the University of Carthage enrolled in (Bachelor’s, Master’s, Doctorate programs).

For programs taught in English:
<https://www.uab.cat/web/studies/undergraduate/undergraduate-offer/subjects-in-english-1345678921142.html>

### Mobility Duration

Spring Term of the 2025/2026 academic year.

### Number of Scholarships

Two Erasmus+ mobility scholarships are offered to eligible students from the University of Carthage.

### Host University

Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona: [www.uab.cat](https://www.uab.cat)

### Eligibility Conditions

- Be enrolled in one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.
- Have a B2 level in English or Spanish.
- Obtain approval for the mobility project from academic supervisors.
- Submit the application within the specified deadline.

### Application Procedure

Interested students must submit their application file to the University of Carthage’s Registry Office before **October 24, 2025**.

### Selection Criteria

- Motivation.
- Academic merit.
- Language skills.

### Application File

- Proof of enrollment.
- Online application form: https://eformularis.uab.cat/web/ari/form-exchange-students-in-erasmus-ka171-project-2024
- Motivation letter.
- Updated CV.
- Language certificate (B2 level).
- Letter of recommendation.
- Academic transcripts.
- Copies of diplomas.
- Valid passport copy.
- Mobility project (Learning Agreement) validated and signed by the Director and Head of Studies or Department Head.
- To receive additional funding, candidates with fewer opportunities or specific inclusion needs must provide an official certification document.

**All requested documents should preferably be written in English.**
