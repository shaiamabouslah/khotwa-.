## Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility KA171 (2024 Project)

### First Round Selection Procedures for Incoming Students

**Education / Traineeship / Short-Term Doctoral Traineeship**

### Education Mobility

- 2 to 5 months student mobility for studies (SMS).
- Financed by the SDU KA171 budget.

### Traineeship Mobility

- Internship mobility may include research activities of doctoral candidates.
- 2 to 5 months student mobility for traineeship (SMP).
- Financed by the SDU KA171 budget.

### Short-Term Doctoral Mobility

- Doctoral candidates can carry out short-term study or internship mobility abroad.
- 5 to 30 days of mobility.
- Financed by the SDU KA171 budget.

### Scholarship

- Education / Traineeship: **800 € per month**.
- Short-Term Doctoral Mobility:
  - 5–14 days: 79 €/day
  - 15–30 days: 56 €/day
- One-time travel grant depending on distance (see table below).
