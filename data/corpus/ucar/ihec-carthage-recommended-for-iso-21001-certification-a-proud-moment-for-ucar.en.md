## IHEC Carthage Recommended for ISO 21001 Certification – A Proud Moment for UCAR!

The University of Carthage is proud to share that IHEC Carthage has been officially recommended for ISO 21001 certification, the international standard for Educational Organizations Management Systems (EOMS), following a rigorous external audit conducted on July 9–10, 2025.

This is a moment of great pride for our university community, and a powerful recognition of IHEC Carthage’s unwavering commitment to quality, transparency, and continuous improvement in higher education.

👏 An immense bravo to the entire IHEC Carthage community — institution, administrative staff, students, coordinators, and quality administrators — for their outstanding dedication and collective effort. Your contribution has been essential to this success.

We are proud of this accomplishment, which marks an important step forward. But it is only the beginning. IHEC Carthage now enters a new phase of continuous progress, innovation, and excellence.

The University of Carthage encourages all its institutions to pursue this shared ambition for quality and performance — with pride and determination.
