## The University of Carthage has obtained the ISO 9001:2015 certification.

The University of Carthage is proud to announce that it has obtained ISO 9001:2015 certification, a testament to its commitment to excellence and continuous improvement in its management processes. This prestigious international recognition underscores the university’s dedication to maintaining the highest quality standards in its services while adhering to the most rigorous management and organizational criteria. This milestone represents a significant achievement in reinforcing our position as a leading institution of higher education.
