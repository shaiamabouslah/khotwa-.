## National Engineering School of Bizerte (ENIB) recommended for ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 21001:2018 Certification!

The University of Carthage is pleased to announce that ENIB has been officially recommended for **ISO 9001:2015** and **ISO 21001:2018 certification**, the international standards for quality management and the management of educational organizations (SMOE).

✨ This is a moment of great pride for our entire university community and a strong recognition of ENIB’s continuous commitment to quality, transparency, and continuous improvement in higher education.

👏 A huge congratulations to the entire ENIB community: directors, administrative staff, students, coordinators, and quality managers, for their outstanding dedication and collective effort. Your contribution has been essential to this achievement.

💪 We are proud of this important milestone, which marks the beginning of a new phase of continuous progress, innovation, and excellence.

The University of Carthage encourages all its institutions to pursue this shared ambition of quality and performance with pride and determination.
