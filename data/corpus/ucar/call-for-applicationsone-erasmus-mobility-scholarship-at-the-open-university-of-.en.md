## Call for applications One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship at the Open University of Cyprus

**Call for applications**
**One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship at the Open University of Cyprus**

**Call for applications**
**One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship at the Open University of Cyprus**

**Program Overview**
The Erasmus+ program allows participants to undertake a mobility period at another institution abroad in order to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

**Target Audience:**
Academic staff from the **University of Carthage** specialized in the following fields:

- Cognitive Systems
- Educational Leadership and Policy
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Master of Business Administration (MBA)
- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) *(from the Spring semester 2025–2026)*
- Security and Defence *(from the Spring semester 2025–2026)*

**Mobility Duration:**
5 days during the 2025/2026 academic year.

**Number of Scholarships:**
One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is offered for the target audience from the University of Carthage.

**Host University:**
Courses that can be offered at the **Open University of Cyprus** can be found at the following link:
<https://www.ouc.ac.cy/index.php/en/studies>

**Eligibility Requirements:**

- Be a faculty member at one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.
- Have a **B2 level in English**.
- Provide a **mobility plan**.
- Submit the application file within the specified deadline.

**Application Procedure:**
Interested teachers must submit their application files to the **Registry Office of the University of Carthage** before **October 24, 2025**.

**Selection Criteria:**

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language proficiency

**Application File:**

- Employment certificate
- Motivation letter
- Updated CV
- Copy of a valid passport
- Mobility project (**Teaching Agreement**) validated and signed

*All required documents must be written in English.*
