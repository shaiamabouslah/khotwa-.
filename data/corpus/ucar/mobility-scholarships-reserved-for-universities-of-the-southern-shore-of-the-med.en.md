## Mobility Scholarships Reserved for Universities of the Southern Shore of the Mediterranean PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT "SARDEGNA FORMED"

**PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT “SARDEGNA FORMED”**

**Call for Applications Reserved for Universities of the Southern Shore of the Mediterranean**
towards the Italian universities of Cagliari and Sassari.

**OBJECTIVES:**
“SARDEGNA FORMED” (Sardinia for the Mediterranean) is a project aimed at promoting international cooperation between higher education institutions of the southern shore of the Mediterranean and Sardinia, in order to ensure the mobility of students from Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia to the Universities of Cagliari and Sassari.

The overall objective of “SARDEGNA FORMED” is to foster excellence in the higher education sector, enhance the recognition of academic studies and degrees, and train highly qualified young men and women capable of responding to the challenges of globalization and the emerging knowledge society.

In this context, the Sardinian Foundation has funded mobility scholarships for the benefit of Algerian, Moroccan, and Tunisian students, with the aim of enabling students from the southern shore of the Mediterranean to pursue academic experiences abroad.
