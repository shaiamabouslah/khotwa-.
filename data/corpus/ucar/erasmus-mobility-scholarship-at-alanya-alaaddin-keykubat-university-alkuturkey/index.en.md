## ERASMUS+ MOBILITY SCHOLARSHIP AT ALANYA ALAADDIN KEYKUBAT UNIVERSITY (ALKU)(Turkey)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program allows students to undertake a mobility period at a foreign institution in order to acquire new skills. This mobility is recognized and validated by the home institution.

### Target Audience

- Students enrolled in a **PhD program** at the **University of Carthage** in the following fields:
  *Economics, Business Administration, Tourism Management, Food Engineering, English Language and Literature*

### Duration of the Mobility

Five months during the **2025/2026 academic year**.

### Number of Scholarships

**One** Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is available for students from the **University of Carthage**.

### Host University

**Alanya Alaaddin Keykubat University (ALKU):** <https://alanya.edu.tr/>

### Eligibility Criteria

- Be enrolled in one of the institutions of the **University of Carthage**.
- Have a **B2 level in English and/or Turkish**.
- Have the mobility project approved by academic advisors.
- Submit the application within the specified deadline.

### Application Procedure

Interested candidates must submit their application **to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage** no later than **July 25, 2025**.

### Selection Criteria

- **Motivation**
- **Academic merit**
- **Language proficiency**

### Application File

- Certificate of enrollment.
- Motivation letter.
- Updated CV.
- Language certificate (B2 level).
- One letter of recommendation.
- Academic transcripts (starting from high school diploma).
- Copies of diplomas.
- Copy of a valid passport.
- **Learning Agreement** (mobility project) approved and signed by the Director and the Head of Studies or Department Head.
