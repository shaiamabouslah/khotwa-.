## AN ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANT AT THE OPEN UNIVERSITY OF CYPRUS

**Program Overview**
The Erasmus+ program allows students to undertake a mobility experience at another institution abroad in order to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

**Target Audience:**

- Undergraduate students (except first-year students) in fields of study/scientific domains covered by Erasmus+ inter-institutional agreements (IIA).
- Master’s students in fields of study/scientific domains covered by Erasmus+ IIAs.

**Duration of Mobility:**
Autumn Term of the 2025/2026 academic year.

**Number of Grants:**
One Erasmus+ mobility grant is available for the target group from the University of Carthage.

**Host University:**
The full list of available study programs can be found here:
<https://www.ouc.ac.cy/index.php/en/studies>

The Open University of Cyprus offers the following master’s programs in English (preferably for Erasmus+ students):

- Master in Business Administration
- Cognitive Systems
- Enterprise Risk Management

**Eligibility Criteria:**

- Be enrolled at one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.
- All incoming students must have at least a B2 level in English (C1 preferred).
- Students interested in study programs offered in Greek will have their application/study plan evaluated and approved beforehand by the academic coordinator.
- The mobility plan must be validated by the administrative authorities.
- Submit the application within the specified deadlines.

**Application Procedure:**
Interested students must submit their application files to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage no later than **May 9, 2025**.

**Selection Criteria:**

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language skills

**Application File:**

- Certificate of enrollment
- Motivation letter
- Updated CV
- Language certificate (minimum level B2)
- One letter of recommendation
- Academic transcripts (starting from high school diploma)
- Copies of diplomas
- Copy of a valid passport
- Mobility project (Learning Agreement) validated and signed by the Director and Head of Studies or Department Head
