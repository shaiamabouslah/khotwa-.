## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS FOR AN ERASMUS+ MOBILITY SCHOLARSHIP AT ÇUKUROVA UNIVERSITY (Turkey)

### Program Overview

The Erasmus+ program allows students to undertake a mobility period at a partner institution abroad to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

### Target Audience

Students enrolled at the University of Carthage in **Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD programs** in the field of **Agriculture, Biological and Related Sciences (Biotechnology), or Fisheries**.

### Mobility Duration

**Fall or Spring Term** of the **2025/2026 academic year**.

### Number of Scholarships

**Only one Erasmus+ mobility scholarship** is available for students from the University of Carthage.

### Host University

**Çukurova University**
Website: <https://www.cu.edu.tr/>

### Eligibility Criteria

- Be enrolled in one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.
- Have at least a **B2 level in English**.
- Have the mobility project approved by academic supervisors.
- Submit the complete application file **within the deadline**.

### Application Procedure

Interested students must submit their application files to the **Registry Office of the University of Carthage** by **May 30, 2025**.

### Selection Criteria

- Motivation
- Academic merit
- Language proficiency

### Application File

- Proof of enrollment
- Motivation letter
- Updated CV
- Language certificate (TOEFL, minimum B2 level)
- One letter of recommendation
- Academic transcripts (from the Baccalaureate onward)
- Copies of diplomas
- Copy of valid passport
- **Mobility project (Learning Agreement)** approved and signed by the Director and Director of Studies or Department Head
