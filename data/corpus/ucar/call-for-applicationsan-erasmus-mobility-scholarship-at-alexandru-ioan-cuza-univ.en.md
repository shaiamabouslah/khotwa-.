## Call for applicationsan erasmus+ mobility scholarship at alexandru ioan cuza university of iasi (romania)

**Program Overview**
The Erasmus+ program allows students to carry out a mobility period in another higher education institution abroad in order to acquire new skills. This mobility is validated and recognized by the home institution.

**Target Audience:**
Students enrolled at the University of Carthage in (Bachelor, Master, PhD) programs in the following fields: 023 Languages; 031 Social and Behavioural Sciences; 041 Business and Administration; 042 Law; 051 Biological and Related Sciences; 052 Environment; 053 Physical Sciences; 054 Mathematics and Statistics; 061 Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs); 1015 Travel, Tourism and Leisure.

**Mobility Duration:**
Students: Spring Term (February 16 – June 21, 2026) of the academic year 2025/2026.

**Academic Calendar:**
<https://www.uaic.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Academic-calendar-25-26.pdf>

**Course Catalogue:**
<https://www.uaic.ro/en/international/international-students/course-catalog-for-incoming-students/>

**Number of Scholarships:**
One Erasmus+ mobility scholarship is offered for students from the University of Carthage.

**Eligibility Criteria**
Student applicants must:

- Be enrolled in one of the institutions of the University of Carthage.
- Have at least a B2 level in English.
- Have their mobility project validated by academic supervisors.
- Submit the application file within the given deadline.

  **Application Procedure**

Interested students must submit their application files to the Registry Office of the University of Carthage no later than 09/10/2025.
