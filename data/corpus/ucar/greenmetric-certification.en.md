## GreenMetric Certification

## University of Carthage Ranked #1 in Tunisia in UI GreenMetric 2025

The University of Carthage has been distinguished in the **UI GreenMetric World University Rankings**, which evaluates higher education institutions based on their commitment to environmental sustainability and eco-friendly practices.

It is ranked as follows:

- **1st in Tunisia**
- **22nd in Africa**
- **688th worldwide**

This recognition reflects the university’s continuous efforts to implement responsible environmental policies, improve resource management, reduce the campus ecological footprint, and promote awareness of sustainable development among students and staff.

Université de Carthage thus confirms its commitment to a quality and environmental approach aligned with international standards, strengthening its position among African and global universities engaged in the ecological transition.
