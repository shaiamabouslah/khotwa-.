## CALL FOR APPLICATIONS – TWO ERASMUS+ MOBILITY GRANTS AT ALEXANDRU IOAN CUZA UNIVERSITY OF IAȘI (ROMANIA)

The University of Carthage is pleased to announce the opening of applications for **two Erasmus+ mobility grants** at the Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași, Romania.

🎓 **Who can apply?**
Students enrolled at the University of Carthage (Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD levels) in eligible fields of study.

📅 **Application deadline:** 11 June 2026

📍 **Mobility duration:** One week

Applicants must demonstrate academic merit, motivation, and English language proficiency (minimum B2 level).
