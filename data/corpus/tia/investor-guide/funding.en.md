## Funding

## Funding mechanisms

The Tunisian funding system includes a wide range of financial support instruments that cover all stages of development of a company and all activity sectors. These instruments are involved in the whole process, from the idea of the project to market distribution through the creation of the company, the operation and the development until reaching international markets.

[The main funding mechanisms](https://guide.tia.gov.tn/uploads/Mecanismes_de_Financement-en.pdf)

For more information, please consult our partners’ platforms which provide an exhaustive overview of the funding mechanisms available.
