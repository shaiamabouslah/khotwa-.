## Step By Step Process

## The overall process Of A Project Development is described as follows

1. Market IntelligenceBefore you decide on a new investment in Tunisia, we can address all your relevant questions related to
market(s) and sectors, benchmarks and countries competitive positioning as well as business environment and
opportunities.2. AuthorisationIn case the activity is subject to a licence, the investor should request the necessary licence from the
concerned administrations in accordance with the provisions of government decree n ° 2018-417 of May 11,
2018. relating to the publication of the exclusive list of economic activities subject to licencing and of
the list of administrative licences required for the implementation of projects, the related provisions and
their simplification.3. Site SelectionTogether with our stakeholders, we team up to fully apprehend the details of your project.
Then, based on your location factors, we identify and present potential sites, so you can select the best
fitted option for your project.4. Business IncorporationWe assist you to remotely carry out all necessary formalities to complete your company's Legal
Incorporation.5. IncentivesWe provide details and assist you with your request for obtaining incentives such as regional
development, R&D incentives, and incentives for national interest projects.6. Customer Care ServicesWe follow up on your project and support your company after the completion and inauguration of your
project in Tunisia, as well as during the ramp up of your operations.1

Before you decide on a new investment in Tunisia, we can address all your relevant questions related to market(s) and sectors, benchmarks and countries competitive positioning as well as business environment and opportunities.

In case the activity is subject to a licence, the investor should request the necessary licence from the concerned administrations in accordance with the provisions of government decree n ° 2018-417 of May 11, 2018. relating to the publication of the exclusive list of economic activities subject to licencing and of the list of administrative licences required for the implementation of projects, the related provisions and their simplification.

Together with our stakeholders, we team up to fully apprehend the details of your project.
Then, based on your location factors, we identify and present potential sites, so you can select the best fitted option for your project.

We assist you to remotely carry out all necessary formalities to complete your company's Legal Incorporation.

We provide details and assist you with your request for obtaining incentives such as regional development, R&D incentives, and incentives for national interest projects.

We follow up on your project and support your company after the completion and inauguration of your project in Tunisia, as well as during the ramp up of your operations.
