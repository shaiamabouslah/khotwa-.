ICM in the new Erasmus+ programme:
geographical targets and restrictions (I)
• Apart from budgetary prioritisation, the development status of partner countries
affects also the eligible flows for student mobility:
• For partnerships with developing countries, only incoming mobilities and outgoing PhD and
staff mobilities are considered eligible (= contributing to the development assistance of the
recipient countries).
• Restrictions for outgoing short, 1st and 2nd cycle students affect certain countries in
almost all regions covered under NDICI (Western Balkans – Region 1 under IPA III is
exempted)
• The restriction apply on countries of the OECD ODA list: https://www.oecd.org/dac/financing-
sustainable-development/development-finance-standards/DAC-List-ODA-Recipients-for-
reporting-2021-flows.pdf

ICM in the new Erasmus+ programme:
geographical targets and restrictions (IV)
• Restricted flows under ICM may be complemented by the international
opening of KA131, based on the strategy and budget of each
beneficiary:
• KA131 => up to 20% of project grant for international mobility
• One budget for the whole world, a wide geographical diversity of destination
countries is encouraged
• Funding only outgoing mobility
• Selection of partner countries and partner HEIs during implementation and not
at application stage.
